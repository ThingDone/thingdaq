Every saved result was reclassified and reconciled against its summary, program, manifest, firmware and artifact hashes. The final cell passed STOP/drain/final-IDLE and idempotent STOP. The service was healthy with an empty queue after qualification.

Both 12-bit ADCs remain at 1 MS/s each; all sixteen GPIO inputs remain at 1 MHz. ADC payload doubles from 506 to 1,012 pairs, and wire frames grow from 2,072 to 4,096 bytes. GPIO frames, rates and Adler-32 are unchanged. At identical sample counts, ADC frame frequency halves; ADC wire cost falls from 4.094862 to 4.047431 bytes/pair (1.1583%). Combined data wire cost falls from 6.118577 to 6.071146 bytes per simultaneous ADC-pair/GPIO-sample (0.7752%). These are layout calculations; received-byte totals also include control traffic and capture/STOP tails.

The CPU field measures GPIO processing only. It does not establish a total CPU or ADC-processing improvement. Queue maxima and temperature come from unequal-length campaigns under different initial thermal conditions, so they do not isolate a causal benefit. Independent checks passed ADC/GPIO/transport health, logical and wire conservation, timestamp continuity, checksums, STOP tails and retention. There was no larger-frame failure to exclude; earlier baseline failures remain preserved in the endurance report.

This is an opt-in experimental layout; the ordinary SDK rejects it. Inputs were electrically unstimulated, so this does not prove fidelity to an external waveform. No GPIO outputs were driven. The successful larger-ADC configuration is the baseline for the next GPIO-only RLE stage. Full counters, lifecycle timings, provenance and hashes are in [the evidence](large-adc600-20261018.json); baseline evidence is in [the endurance report](clock600-ready-endurance-20261018.md).
